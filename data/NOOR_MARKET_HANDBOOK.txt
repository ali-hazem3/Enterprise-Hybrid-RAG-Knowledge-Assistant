NOOR MARKET — COMPANY HANDBOOK
Internal reference, version 1.2, June 2026

ABOUT US
Noor Market is a neighbourhood grocery chain in Dubai founded in 2014.
We operate three branches: Deira, Marina, and Jumeirah. Our promise is
simple: fresh essentials, fair prices, and staff who know your name.

BRANCHES AND MANAGERS
- Deira branch — managed by Omar Al Farsi. Our first and oldest store,
  located near the Gold Souq metro station.
- Marina branch — managed by Huda Salem. Our largest store, with the
  in-house bakery and the widest fresh produce section.
- Jumeirah branch — managed by Ritika Menon. Our newest store, opened
  in 2023, focused on organic and specialty items.

OPENING HOURS
All branches: Saturday to Thursday, 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM.
Friday: 7:00 AM to 11:00 AM, then 1:00 PM to 11:00 PM.
Ramadan hours are announced separately each year.

RETURN POLICY
Non-perishable items can be returned within 14 days with a receipt.
Fresh and chilled items can only be returned on the same day of
purchase. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 3
working days. No returns on items bought during clearance sales.

DELIVERY
Free delivery on orders above AED 100 within our delivery zones.
Orders below AED 100 carry a flat AED 12 delivery fee. Delivery slots
are 2 hours wide; same-day delivery closes at 8:00 PM.

NOOR POINTS (LOYALTY PROGRAM)
Customers earn 1 Noor Point for every AED 10 spent. 100 points can be
redeemed for AED 5 off any purchase. Points expire 12 months after
they are earned. Sign-up is free at any till.

SUPPLIERS AND QUALITY
Fresh produce arrives daily before 6:00 AM. Dairy is restocked twice
daily. Any item within 2 days of expiry is moved to the orange
"Last Chance" shelf at 30% off.
