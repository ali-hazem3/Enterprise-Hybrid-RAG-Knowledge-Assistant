PULSEFIT — MEMBER SERVICES HANDBOOK
Internal reference, version 2.0, June 2026

ABOUT US
PulseFit is a fitness chain in Dubai founded in 2019. We operate three
clubs: Al Barsha, Downtown, and Silicon Oasis. Our promise: serious
training, no judgement, transparent pricing.

CLUBS AND MANAGERS
- Al Barsha club — managed by Faris Al Marri. Our largest club, with
  an Olympic-length pool and the women's-only training floor.
- Downtown club — managed by Yasmin Kassab. Our flagship, focused on
  classes and personal training, two minutes from the metro.
- Silicon Oasis club — managed by Dev Patel. Our newest club, opened
  2024, popular with students and the tech crowd.

OPENING HOURS
Saturday to Thursday: 6:00 AM to 12:00 midnight.
Friday: 6:00 AM to 11:00 AM, then 1:00 PM to 12:00 midnight.
Ladies-only hours at Al Barsha: Sunday and Tuesday, 9:00 AM to 12:00 noon.

MEMBERSHIP FREEZES
Any member can freeze their membership for up to 30 days per calendar
year, minimum 7 days per freeze. Freezes are requested at reception or
by email, and start the following day. Day Pass holders cannot freeze.

PERSONAL TRAINING
PT sessions are booked through the app. Cancelling less than 12 hours
before a session means the session is charged in full. Trainers are
assigned per club and cannot be booked across clubs.

REFUNDS
Annual plans have a 7-day money-back guarantee from the join date, no
questions asked. Monthly plans and Day Passes are non-refundable.
Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5 working
days.

GUESTS
Members may bring a guest for AED 75 per visit. Annual plan members
(Premium Annual and Corporate Annual) get 2 free guest passes per month.
Guests must sign a waiver at reception.

CONTACT
Member care: care@pulsefit.example — replies within one working day.
